Services

Four service lines, one employer

Every Foundling service runs through the same structure. Foundling is the W-2 employer, the client directs the work, and one service agreement and one invoice cover the arrangement.

Employer of record

Foundling hires the worker as its own W-2 employee. From that point Foundling is responsible for the employment relationship and the client is responsible for the work.

Foundling holds
The employment relationship, payroll and employment tax filings, workers compensation coverage, New York State disability coverage, unemployment insurance accounts, and the personnel file.
The client holds
Day to day direction of the work, schedules, worksite standards, equipment, and performance expectations at the site.
The worker gets
A written offer with pay rate and pay schedule, a paycheck from Foundling with taxes withheld, coverage while working, and one employer to call about pay or employment questions.

Staffing and placement

Foundling sources, screens, and places hourly and salaried workers against roles the client defines. Two arrangements are available:

Temp to hire

The worker starts on Foundling's payroll and works at the client site. If both sides want to make it permanent, the client converts the worker to its own payroll on terms set in the service agreement.

Direct placement

Foundling recruits and screens the candidate, and the client hires the worker onto its own payroll from day one. Foundling's role ends at placement unless the client also engages payroll administration.

Screening is matched to the role: work history verification for every placement, plus background and eligibility checks where the position or the worksite requires them.

Payroll and benefits administration

For workers Foundling employs, payroll is not an add-on, it is the core of the service:

  • Payroll processing on the schedule stated in each worker's offer, with tax withholding and direct deposit.
  • Wage and hour recordkeeping, including time records, pay statements, and overtime calculation.
  • PTO accrual tracking and administration under the applicable policy.
  • Benefits enrollment support: explaining what is offered, collecting elections, and administering deductions.

Clients see all of this reflected on one itemized invoice rather than running any of it internally.

Compliance and onboarding

Every worker Foundling employs goes through the same documented onboarding sequence before the first shift:

  1. Employment documents

    I-9 employment eligibility verification and W-4 withholding elections, completed and retained.

  2. Role-specific verification

    Background checks and eligibility or registration verification where the role or the worksite requires them, including state requirements for regulated industries.

  3. Policy acknowledgment

    The worker receives and acknowledges the applicable policies in writing before starting.

  4. Training documentation

    Industry-specific training requirements are documented and kept in the personnel file, so the client and the worker can both show completion.
