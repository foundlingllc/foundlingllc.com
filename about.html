About Foundling

Foundling LLC is a New York limited liability company based in Granville, New York, in Washington County. It provides employer of record, staffing and placement, and payroll administration services to operating companies in New York State.

Why the company exists

Small operating companies need real employees: hired properly, paid on time, insured, and documented. Building the internal function to do that well takes payroll systems, insurance relationships, filing calendars, and recordkeeping discipline that most small operators cannot justify carrying.

Foundling exists to carry that function instead. It employs the workforce, administers everything the employment relationship requires, and bills the client for the service. The client keeps its attention on the operation.

What Foundling is responsible for

  • Foundling hires placed workers as its own W-2 employees.
  • Foundling runs payroll, withholds taxes, and files employment tax returns.
  • Foundling maintains workers compensation and New York State disability coverage for all placed employees.
  • Foundling completes and retains I-9, W-4, policy, and training documentation for every worker.
  • Foundling verifies eligibility and registration where a role or worksite requires it, including state requirements in regulated industries.

How the company is structured

Foundling LLC is organized as a limited liability company under New York law. It contracts with client companies through written service agreements, employs placed workers directly, and operates from Granville, New York. All correspondence runs through the company's role addresses: hello@foundlingllc.com for engagements and careers@foundlingllc.com for applicants.
