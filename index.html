Employer of record, staffing, and payroll administration

Foundling hires, pays, and insures the workforce. Clients direct the work.

Foundling LLC is a New York limited liability company based in Granville, New York. It employs W-2 workers, runs their payroll, files their employment taxes, carries workers compensation and disability coverage, and places them at client companies. The client directs the day to day work and receives one invoice for the whole arrangement.

What Foundling does

Employer of record

Foundling is the legal employer for workers placed at client worksites. It hires, runs payroll, files employment taxes, holds workers compensation and disability coverage, and administers onboarding and compliance documentation.

Employer of record in detail

Staffing and placement

Sourcing, screening, and placing hourly and salaried workers. Temp to hire and direct placement, matched to the client's roles, schedules, and site requirements.

Staffing and placement in detail

Payroll and benefits administration

Payroll processing, wage and hour recordkeeping, PTO administration, and benefits enrollment support, with I-9, W-4, and policy documentation completed and retained for every worker.

How an engagement works

  1. Scope

    The client describes the roles, headcount, worksite, schedules, and pay rates. Foundling confirms what it can staff and administer.

  2. Agreement

    Both parties sign a service agreement that sets bill rates, responsibilities, and terms. The bill rate covers wages, employer taxes, insurance, and Foundling's administration fee.

  3. Hiring and onboarding

    Foundling hires each worker as its own W-2 employee, completes I-9 and W-4 documentation, verifies eligibility where the role requires it, and places the worker at the client site.

  4. Ongoing administration

    Foundling runs payroll on the stated schedule, maintains coverage, keeps wage and hour records, and sends the client one itemized invoice. The client directs the work at the site.
