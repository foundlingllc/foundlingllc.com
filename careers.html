Careers

Work for Foundling, at a worksite near you

Foundling employs people and places them at farms, food producers, manufacturers, and other operating companies in New York. Your employer is Foundling. Your worksite is the client's. This page explains what that means in practice.

What being employed by Foundling means

You are a W-2 employee
Foundling hires you, withholds your taxes, and issues your W-2 at the end of the year. This is not gig work and not independent contracting.
Foundling signs your paycheck
Your pay rate and pay schedule are stated in writing before you start. Pay comes from Foundling on that schedule, with a pay statement showing hours, rate, and withholdings.
You are covered while you work
Foundling carries workers compensation and New York State disability coverage for all placed employees.
The client directs the work
At the worksite, the client's supervisor sets the tasks and the schedule. For anything about pay, employment, or a problem you cannot raise on site, you contact Foundling.

What to expect from onboarding

  1. Apply and talk

    Send your work history to careers@foundlingllc.com. Foundling reviews it and contacts you about roles that fit.

  2. Offer in writing

    Before you start you get the pay rate, pay schedule, worksite, and schedule in writing.

  3. Paperwork before the first shift

    I-9 and W-4 forms, direct deposit setup, policy acknowledgment, and any background or eligibility verification the role requires. Some regulated worksites require state eligibility or registration checks; Foundling completes these with you before you start.

  4. First shift

    You report to the client's supervisor at the worksite. Site training is documented in your file.

Pay and records

  • Time is recorded at the site and approved each pay period.
  • Every paycheck comes with a pay statement showing hours, rate, overtime, and withholdings.
  • PTO, where the role includes it, accrues under a written policy you receive at onboarding.
  • Questions about pay go to Foundling directly, not through the worksite.

How to apply

Email careers@foundlingllc.com with your name, the kind of work you are looking for, the area you can work in, and a resume or a plain description of your work history. A resume is helpful but not required.
