Industries

Where Foundling works

Foundling serves operating companies whose work is physical, scheduled, and regulated: farms, food producers, manufacturers, and licensed operators. The employment structure is the same in every industry. What changes is the screening, the training documentation, and the compliance requirements attached to each role.

Agriculture and greenhouse operations

Seasonal peaks, weather-driven schedules, and physically demanding roles make agricultural payroll harder than it looks. Foundling employs field, greenhouse, and packing workers, manages the wage and hour recordkeeping that agricultural work requires, and gives producers a single predictable bill rate per hour worked instead of a payroll department.

Food and beverage production

Production facilities need reliable shift coverage and documented training. Foundling places and employs line, sanitation, and warehouse workers for food and beverage manufacturers, documents food-safety and site-specific training in each worker's file, and keeps time and attendance records aligned with the client's shift structure.

Light manufacturing and warehouse

For machine operators, assemblers, material handlers, and warehouse staff, Foundling handles sourcing and screening, employs the workers, and administers payroll and PTO. Temp to hire is common here: the client evaluates a worker on Foundling's payroll, then converts the ones it wants to keep.

Regulated industries

Foundling administers employment for workers placed at operators in regulated industries, including licensed New York State cannabis operators. The client holds the license and directs the work at the site. Foundling is the employer of record: it hires the worker, runs payroll, carries workers compensation and disability coverage, and maintains the onboarding and training file.

All placements meet applicable state eligibility and registration requirements, and those verifications are completed and documented before the worker's first shift. For a licensed operator, the point of the arrangement is that employment administration is handled by a party whose records are complete, current, and available for inspection.

Not sure your industry fits

If your company needs W-2 workers and does not want to run payroll, the structure likely applies. Describe the roles and the worksite to hello@foundlingllc.com and Foundling will tell you plainly whether it can serve the engagement.
